# STRATUMX_L7_STUDIO_ORCHESTRATION_CONSTITUTION

## Scope
This constitution governs studio orchestration in `L7`.

## Binding laws
- `L7` owns campaigns, governance, automation meta, and reporting meta;
- orchestration coordinates lower services but does not mutate their hidden truth directly;
- campaign state remains explicit and auditable.

## Audit checks
- project/content/world/simulation/release campaigns are separated;
- meta layers do not impersonate runtime services;
- reporting stays derived from declared lower artifacts and streams.
