# STRATUMX_L6_TOOLING_CONSTITUTION

## Scope
This constitution governs `L6` tooling runtime proper.

## Binding laws
- `L6` owns tooling planes and service runtimes, not product UI ownership;
- mutable authoring truth lives in declared planes only;
- every service publishes request/result or snapshot/index surfaces explicitly.

## Audit checks
- planes remain explicit and non-overlapping;
- service runtimes consume bridge/editor inputs through declared surfaces only;
- no hidden shell or campaign truth leaks into `L6`.
