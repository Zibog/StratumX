# STRATUMX_L6_FAMILY_COMPOSITION_LAW

## Scope
This law governs family composition within tooling.

## Binding laws
- families group related levels for composition, navigation, and audit, not for hidden authority;
- family contracts must state common edges, shared data posture, and shared exclusions;
- family keys and paths must remain unique and mechanically resolvable.

## Audit checks
- family registry matches physical family layout;
- family docs say more than their titles;
- no family becomes a vague dumping ground for unrelated services.
