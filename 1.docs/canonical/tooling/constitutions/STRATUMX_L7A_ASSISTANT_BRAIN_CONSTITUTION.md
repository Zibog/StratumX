# STRATUMX_L7A_ASSISTANT_BRAIN_CONSTITUTION

## Scope
This constitution governs `L7A` assistant intelligence/planning.

## Binding laws
- `L7A` owns understanding, planning, canon reasoning, generation, optimization, migration, and routing;
- `L7A` may reason over lower evidence but may not seize lower authority;
- planner outputs remain proposals or orchestration inputs until accepted below.

## Audit checks
- plan/goal/reasoning layers remain explicit;
- model/planner semantics do not leak into bridge or tooling runtime truth;
- evidence used by `L7A` is declared and traceable.
