# STRATUMX_L6_NON_INTERFERENCE_LAW

## Scope
This law prevents tooling from interfering with adjacent strata.

## Binding laws
- tooling may not seize editor product ownership or assistant brain ownership;
- tooling may not rewrite engine truth outside legal request/result contracts;
- families and sidecars must remain narrow enough to compose rather than dominate adjacent layers.

## Audit checks
- workspace/runtime docs do not claim shell ownership;
- assistant and campaign semantics stay in their own strata;
- local packs avoid hidden cross-layer stores.
