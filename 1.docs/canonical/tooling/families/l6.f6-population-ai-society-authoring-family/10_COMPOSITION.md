# Composition

- population, AI, and society authoring graphs and scenario authoring
- authority-facing minimal truth: population/AI edit intents
- snapshot classes: population and AI snapshots
- index classes: AI lookup indices
- derived classes: derived AI/society views

## Composition rule
The family exists to make domain adjacency for `population_ai_society_authoring_family` explicit, not to merge members into one truth owner.
