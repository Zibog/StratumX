# Family Local Fields

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f1 content family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Coordination records for `content_family`
- coordination record for content browser, asset identity, import/reimport flows, dependency and reverse-reference views, content manifests
- coordination record for authority-facing minimal truth: minimal asset refs and edit intents
- coordination record for snapshot classes: content snapshots
- coordination record for index classes: content lookup, dependency, reverse-dependency, and search indices
- coordination record for derived classes: filtered views, thumbnails, diff summaries

## Field note
Each local coordination record exists only to describe family locality and activation for these members.
