# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f1 content family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `content_family`
- member-local coordination for content browser, asset identity, import/reimport flows, dependency and reverse-reference views, content manifests
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: minimal asset refs and edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: content snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: content lookup, dependency, reverse-dependency, and search indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: filtered views, thumbnails, diff summaries
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
