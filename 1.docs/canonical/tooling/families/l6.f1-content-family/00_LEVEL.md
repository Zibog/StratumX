# Content Family

Canonical family: `content_family`

## Composes
- content browser, asset identity, import/reimport flows, dependency and reverse-reference views, content manifests
- authority-facing minimal truth: minimal asset refs and edit intents
- snapshot classes: content snapshots
- index classes: content lookup, dependency, reverse-dependency, and search indices
- derived classes: filtered views, thumbnails, diff summaries

## Family law
`content_family` coordinates locality, activation, and dependency posture for its member levels without replacing their authority or record ownership.
