# Data Responsibility

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f1 content family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-scoped coordination for `content_family`
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around content browser, asset identity, import/reimport flows, dependency and reverse-reference views, content manifests
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around authority-facing minimal truth: minimal asset refs and edit intents
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around snapshot classes: content snapshots
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around index classes: content lookup, dependency, reverse-dependency, and search indices
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around derived classes: filtered views, thumbnails, diff summaries

## Responsibility note
These coordination classes describe family behavior and do not replace member truth ownership.
