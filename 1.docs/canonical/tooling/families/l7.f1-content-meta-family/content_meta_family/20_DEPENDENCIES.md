# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l7.f1 content meta family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `content_meta_family`
- member-local coordination for content campaign composition and content workflow meta
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: content campaign refs only
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: content-campaign snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: content-campaign indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived content campaign views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
