# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f10 audio family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-local communication for `audio_family`
- coordination and refresh propagation related to audio authoring, buses, routing views, and audio manifests
- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: audio edit intents
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: audio snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: audio lookup indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: derived audio views

## Note
The family coordinates these member concerns without introducing a new authority path.
