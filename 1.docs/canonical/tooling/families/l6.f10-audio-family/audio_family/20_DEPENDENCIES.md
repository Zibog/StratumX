# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f10 audio family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `audio_family`
- member-local coordination for audio authoring, buses, routing views, and audio manifests
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: audio edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: audio snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: audio lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived audio views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
