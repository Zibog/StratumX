# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f3 world family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `world_family`
- member-local coordination for world composition, world-part selection, data layers, and world-state views
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: world edit intents and active world refs
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: world snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: world lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived world views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
