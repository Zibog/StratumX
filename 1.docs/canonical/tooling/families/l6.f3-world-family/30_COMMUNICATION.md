# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f3 world family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-local communication for `world_family`
- coordination and refresh propagation related to world composition, world-part selection, data layers, and world-state views
- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: world edit intents and active world refs
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: world snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: world lookup indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: derived world views

## Note
The family coordinates these member concerns without introducing a new authority path.
