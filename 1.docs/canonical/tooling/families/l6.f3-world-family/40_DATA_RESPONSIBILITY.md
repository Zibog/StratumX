# Data Responsibility

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f3 world family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-scoped coordination for `world_family`
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around world composition, world-part selection, data layers, and world-state views
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around authority-facing minimal truth: world edit intents and active world refs
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around snapshot classes: world snapshots
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around index classes: world lookup indices
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around derived classes: derived world views

## Responsibility note
These coordination classes describe family behavior and do not replace member truth ownership.
