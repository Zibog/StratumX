# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l7a.f0 assistant intelligence family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `assistant_intelligence_family`
- member-local coordination for goal understanding, planning, canon reasoning, optimization, migration, and routing composition
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: goal/plan refs only
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: planning snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: planning lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived plan and reasoning views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
