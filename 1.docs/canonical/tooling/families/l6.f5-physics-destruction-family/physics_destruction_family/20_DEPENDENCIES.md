# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f5 physics destruction family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `physics_destruction_family`
- member-local coordination for physics/destruction authoring, structural constraints, and destruction diagnostics
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: physics/destruction edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: physics snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: physics lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived physics views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
