# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f5 physics destruction family and describes family-only coordination.

- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: physics/destruction edit intents
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: physics snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: physics lookup indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: derived physics views

## Note
The family coordinates these member concerns without introducing a new authority path.
