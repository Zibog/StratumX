# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f13 observability diagnostics family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-local communication for `observability_diagnostics_family`
- coordination and refresh propagation related to observability views, logs, probes, metrics dashboards, validation visibility, and diagnostics routing
- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: observability-session refs only
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: observability snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: diagnostics lookup indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: dashboards, summaries, and remediation views

## Note
The family coordinates these member concerns without introducing a new authority path.
