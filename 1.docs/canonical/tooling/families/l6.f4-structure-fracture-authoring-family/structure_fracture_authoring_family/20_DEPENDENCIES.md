# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f4 structure fracture authoring family family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `structure_fracture_authoring_family`
- member-local coordination for fracture authoring, fracture rule sets, and fracture preview routing
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: fracture edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: fracture snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: fracture lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived fracture views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
