# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f9 acoustics voice authoring family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `acoustics_voice_authoring_family`
- member-local coordination for acoustics and voice authoring, emitters, and propagation authoring views
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: acoustics/voice edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: acoustics snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: acoustics lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived acoustics views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
