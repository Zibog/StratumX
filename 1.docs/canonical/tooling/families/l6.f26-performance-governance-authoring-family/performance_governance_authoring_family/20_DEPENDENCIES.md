# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f11 performance governance authoring family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `performance_governance_authoring_family`
- member-local coordination for performance governance authoring, budget policies, and degradation policy authoring
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: budget/governance edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: budget/governance snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: policy lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived governance views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
