# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f4 material surface family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `material_surface_family`
- member-local coordination for surface authoring, material layering, and surface instance views
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: surface edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: surface snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: surface lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived surface views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
