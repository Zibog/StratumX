# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f4 material surface family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-local communication for `material_surface_family`
- coordination and refresh propagation related to surface authoring, material layering, and surface instance views
- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: surface edit intents
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: surface snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: surface lookup indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: derived surface views

## Note
The family coordinates these member concerns without introducing a new authority path.
