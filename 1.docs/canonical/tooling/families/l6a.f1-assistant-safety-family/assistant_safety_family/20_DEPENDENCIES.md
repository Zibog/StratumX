# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6a.f1 assistant safety family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `assistant_safety_family`
- member-local coordination for assistant safety, approval, legality, and apply/revert policy composition
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: safety/approval refs only
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: safety snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: safety lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived safety views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
