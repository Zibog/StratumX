# Composition

## Member composition
- acoustics and voice authoring, emitters, and propagation authoring views
- authority-facing minimal truth: acoustics/voice edit intents
- snapshot classes: acoustics snapshots
- index classes: acoustics lookup indices
- derived classes: derived acoustics views

## Composition rule
The family exists to make domain adjacency for `acoustics_voice_authoring_family` explicit, not to merge members into one truth owner.
