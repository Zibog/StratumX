# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f4 structure fracture authoring family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-local communication for `structure_fracture_authoring_family`
- coordination and refresh propagation related to fracture authoring, fracture rule sets, and fracture preview routing
- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: fracture edit intents
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: fracture snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: fracture lookup indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: derived fracture views

## Note
The family coordinates these member concerns without introducing a new authority path.
