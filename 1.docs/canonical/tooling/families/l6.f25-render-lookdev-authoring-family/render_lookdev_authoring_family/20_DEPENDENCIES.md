# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f10 render lookdev authoring family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `render_lookdev_authoring_family`
- member-local coordination for render lookdev authoring, lighting/look rules, and render previews
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: lookdev edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: lookdev snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: lookdev lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived lookdev views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
