# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f12 automation family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-local communication for `automation_family`
- coordination and refresh propagation related to automation editor views, automation specs, and automation diagnostics
- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: automation edit intents
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: automation snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: automation lookup indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: derived automation views

## Note
The family coordinates these member concerns without introducing a new authority path.
