# Animation Motion Authoring Family

Canonical family: `animation_motion_authoring_family`

## Composes
- motion authoring, clip graphs, motion rules, and motion previews
- authority-facing minimal truth: motion edit intents
- snapshot classes: motion snapshots
- index classes: motion lookup indices
- derived classes: derived motion views

## Family law
`animation_motion_authoring_family` coordinates locality, activation, and dependency posture for its member levels without replacing their authority or record ownership.
