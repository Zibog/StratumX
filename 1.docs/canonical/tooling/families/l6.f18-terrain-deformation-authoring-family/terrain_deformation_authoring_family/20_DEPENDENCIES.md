# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f3 terrain deformation authoring family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `terrain_deformation_authoring_family`
- member-local coordination for terrain layers, deformation intents, brush results, and terrain manifests
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: terrain edit intents only
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: terrain snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: terrain indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: terrain-derived overlays
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
