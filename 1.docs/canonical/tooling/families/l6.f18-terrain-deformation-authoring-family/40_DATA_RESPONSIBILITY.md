# Data Responsibility

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f3 terrain deformation authoring family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-scoped coordination for `terrain_deformation_authoring_family`
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around terrain layers, deformation intents, brush results, and terrain manifests
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around authority-facing minimal truth: terrain edit intents only
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around snapshot classes: terrain snapshots
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around index classes: terrain indices
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around derived classes: terrain-derived overlays

## Responsibility note
These coordination classes describe family behavior and do not replace member truth ownership.
