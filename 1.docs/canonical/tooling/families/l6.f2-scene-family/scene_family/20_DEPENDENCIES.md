# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f2 scene family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `scene_family`
- member-local coordination for scene graph authoring, entity grouping, scene views, and scene manifests
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: scene edit intents and scene root refs
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: scene snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: scene/spatial lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived scene views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
