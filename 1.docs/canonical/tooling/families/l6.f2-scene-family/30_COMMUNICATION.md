# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f2 scene family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-local communication for `scene_family`
- coordination and refresh propagation related to scene graph authoring, entity grouping, scene views, and scene manifests
- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: scene edit intents and scene root refs
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: scene snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: scene/spatial lookup indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: derived scene views

## Note
The family coordinates these member concerns without introducing a new authority path.
