This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f2 scene family and describes family-only coordination.

- locality, activation, and diagnostics around snapshot classes: scene snapshots
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around index classes: scene/spatial lookup indices
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around derived classes: derived scene views
