# Cinematic Family

Canonical family: `cinematic_family`

## Composes
- timeline authoring, shot views, track bindings, event markers, camera rigs, and cinematic manifests
- authority-facing minimal truth: cinematic edit intents and binding refs
- snapshot classes: cinematic snapshots
- index classes: shot, track, and binding lookup indices
- derived classes: cinematic timelines, shot lists, and blend graphs

## Family law
`cinematic_family` coordinates locality, activation, and dependency posture for its member levels without replacing their authority or record ownership.
