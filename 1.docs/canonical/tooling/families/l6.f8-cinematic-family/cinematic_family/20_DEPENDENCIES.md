# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f8 cinematic family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `cinematic_family`
- member-local coordination for timeline authoring, shot views, track bindings, event markers, camera rigs, and cinematic manifests
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: cinematic edit intents and binding refs
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: cinematic snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: shot, track, and binding lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: cinematic timelines, shot lists, and blend graphs
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
