# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f7 combat ballistics damage authoring family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `combat_ballistics_damage_authoring_family`
- member-local coordination for combat, ballistics, and damage authoring graphs and tuning rules
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: combat/ballistics edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: combat snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: combat lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived combat views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
