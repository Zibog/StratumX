# Data Responsibility

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f9 vfx family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-scoped coordination for `vfx_family`
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around VFX authoring, effect graphs, effect views, and VFX manifests
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around authority-facing minimal truth: VFX edit intents
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around snapshot classes: VFX snapshots
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around index classes: VFX lookup indices
- locality, activation, and diagnostics around derived classes: derived VFX views

## Responsibility note
These coordination classes describe family behavior and do not replace member truth ownership.
