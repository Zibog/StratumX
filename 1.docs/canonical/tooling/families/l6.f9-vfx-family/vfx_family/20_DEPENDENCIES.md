# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f9 vfx family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `vfx_family`
- member-local coordination for VFX authoring, effect graphs, effect views, and VFX manifests
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: VFX edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: VFX snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: VFX lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived VFX views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
