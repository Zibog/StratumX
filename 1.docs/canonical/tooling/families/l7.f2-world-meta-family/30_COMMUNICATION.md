# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l7.f2 world meta family and describes family-only coordination.

- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: world campaign refs only
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: world-campaign snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: world-campaign indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: derived world campaign views

## Note
