# Family Local Dependencies

This local family contract belongs specifically to the l6.f8 animation motion authoring family family and may not be reused by a different family key.


## Allowed for `animation_motion_authoring_family`
- member-local coordination for motion authoring, clip graphs, motion rules, and motion previews
- member-local coordination for authority-facing minimal truth: motion edit intents
- member-local coordination for snapshot classes: motion snapshots
- member-local coordination for index classes: motion lookup indices
- member-local coordination for derived classes: derived motion views
- package-root family registry and shared ids
- lower packages only through member-legal surfaces

## Forbidden
- undeclared member truth
- unrelated domain truth
