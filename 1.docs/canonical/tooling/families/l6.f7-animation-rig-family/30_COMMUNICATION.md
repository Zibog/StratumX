# Communication

This contract belongs specifically to the l6.f7 animation rig family and describes family-only coordination.


## Family-local communication for `animation_rig_family`
- coordination and refresh propagation related to rig authoring, rig refs, rig validation, and rig manifests
- coordination and refresh propagation related to authority-facing minimal truth: rig edit intents
- coordination and refresh propagation related to snapshot classes: rig snapshots
- coordination and refresh propagation related to index classes: rig lookup indices
- coordination and refresh propagation related to derived classes: derived rig views

## Note
The family coordinates these member concerns without introducing a new authority path.
