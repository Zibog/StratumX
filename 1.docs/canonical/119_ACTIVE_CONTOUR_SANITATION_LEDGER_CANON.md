## Keep law
A document stays active only if it contributes one of the following:
- unique ownership law;
- unique field or packet tables;
- unique route stages or recovery duties;
- unique operator controls or diagnostics;
- unique readiness, acceptance, or truth accounting.

## Delete law
A document may be deleted only when its function is fully covered by another active document and the replacement is named here or in a successor sanitation ledger.

## Future sanitation rule
No future patch wave may reintroduce duplicate active ordinals.
History must be appended under `canonical_history/`, not reinserted into the active contour.
