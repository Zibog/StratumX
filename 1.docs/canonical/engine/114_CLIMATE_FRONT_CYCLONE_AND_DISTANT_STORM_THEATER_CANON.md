# Climate Front Cyclone And Distant Storm Theater Canon

**Stack version:** `SX-CANON/1.0.24/STACK-v30`

## Purpose
Own theater-scale climate evolution so distant storms, fronts, and lunar-coupled night conditions remain lawful and replayable.

## Exact truth objects
- `ClimateCellSet`
- `WeatherFrontTrackSet`
- `CycloneAnticycloneLedger`
- `PrecipitationFieldSummary`
- `LightningEventTrack`
- `LunarCycleLedger`
- `ClimateReplayAnchor`

## Lifecycle registry
`forming -> stable -> intensifying -> splitting / merging -> decaying -> dissipated`

## Distant-observability law
A storm visible at `30–200 km` must publish one far track and one observability digest even when exact local cloud truth is absent.

## Deterministic replay law
Climate evolution may use summarized carriers and seeded variation, but replay must reproduce:
- front identity;
- arrival window;
- precipitation class changes;
- lightning event ordering;
- lunar phase and moon-light context.

## Failure families
- `climate.front_identity_drift`
- `climate.observability_fraud`
- `climate.lightning_event_gap`
- `climate.replay_anchor_missing`
