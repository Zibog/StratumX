# Distant World Representation And Causal Degradation Canon

**Stack version:** `SX-CANON/1.0.24/STACK-v30`

## Purpose
Freeze exact representational classes for the far world.

## Representation classes
- `live_exact`
- `reduced_exact`
- `statistical_far`
- `retained_summary`
- `impostor_world`

## Distance law
| Band | What may survive |
|---|---|
| `0–300 m` | exact local truth or reduced-exact by ring contract |
| `300 m–3 km` | summary of fires, structures, squads, migration fronts, weather, and major lights |
| `3–30 km` | retained summary and theater observability only |
| `30–200 km` | climate, skyline lights, major fire plumes, large migration/theater carriers |

## Causality-preserving degradation law
Degradation may reduce richness, cadence, and detail; it may not erase the cause chain for anything the player can lawfully perceive.
