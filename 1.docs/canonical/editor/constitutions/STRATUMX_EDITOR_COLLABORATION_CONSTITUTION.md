# STRATUMX_EDITOR_COLLABORATION_CONSTITUTION

## Scope
This constitution governs collaboration, review, annotation, asset approval, playtest, and production traceability surfaces.

## Binding laws
- collaboration surfaces are request/view surfaces above lower truth;
- review annotations, approvals, and production dashboards must remain auditable and non-destructive;
- collaborative workflow state may not become hidden side authority over engine/tooling truth.

## Audit checks
- `L11` surfaces state ownership, review flow, and traceability outputs explicitly;
- collaboration does not bypass validation/build/release law;
- production-facing surfaces remain bounded and reversible.
