# Material Centric Operator Surface Canon

**Stack version:** `SX-CANON/1.0.24/STACK-v30`

## Purpose
Freeze one explicit material-centric operator surface so the editor no longer spreads material-owned authoring across labs without a canonical center.

## Surface law
The material-centric operator surface is mandatory for any project that uses material law as the owner of physical, visual, audio, light, runtime, persistence, and proof response.

This surface may open domain-specific previews, but it remains one grammar space.

## Territory-aware surface law
The same surface must author all material territories through one grammar.
It must not fork into incompatible editor religions for:
- landscape / roads / terrain;
- vegetation / trees / shrubs / grass;
- built structure / walls / floors / roofs;
- props / furniture / containers / doors;
- living surfaces / skin / tissue / layered cloth-and-armor stacks.

The surrounding labs may differ, but the material law language must remain one.

## Normalized preview vocabulary law
Friendly operator wording is allowed, but it must map one-way to root trigger classes:
- `Preview Contact` -> `trigger.contact`
- `Preview Bullet Hit` -> `trigger.ballistic_hit`
- `Preview Blast` -> `trigger.blast_overpressure`
- `Preview Burn` -> `trigger.fire_exposure`
- `Preview Wetness` -> `trigger.wetness_contact`
- `Preview Light Exposure` -> `trigger.light_exposure`

The editor may not let surface prose drift into an unnormalized second vocabulary.

## Mandatory focus law
- successful binding keeps focus in the material-centric surface and updates preview panes in place;
- branch-specific labs may open as secondary views but may not steal ownership of the underlying material grammar;
- missing-binding or incompatibility failures must focus the exact blocking subsection, not a generic lab home;
- branch-coverage inspection used for freeze review must retain evidence when root proof law marks retained artifacts mandatory.

## Living and coverage law
This surface may bind living-host or coverage-related package branches, but it may not swallow the ownership of:
- living traversal and wound/species consequence from engine `105` and editor `72`;
- fur/hair promoted-local richness diagnostics from engine `106` and editor `68`.

The material-centric surface is the grammar center, not the runtime owner of every downstream system.

## Shell-position law
The material-centric surface is one stage-adjacent center inside the larger editor shell.
It must open cleanly from the canonical Workspace/World stage and remain reachable from the main stage strip without lab-hunting.


## Cross-domain exits from the material-centric surface
The material-centric surface must expose lawful exits to:
- terrain material assignment when a terrain layer schema is affected;
- destruction topology lab when fracture/collapse behavior depends on material truth;
- hydrology lab when wetness/leak/freeze behavior depends on material truth;
- wound/species lab when layered traversal families depend on material truth;
- fur/groom lab when coverage/wetness/char response depends on material truth.

These exits may be secondary surfaces.
They may not force the operator to abandon material-owned identity and reason chains.
