# World Gameplay Authoring And Validation Lab Canon

**Stack version:** `SX-CANON/1.0.24/STACK-v30`

## Purpose
Apply world deltas, author gameplay, validate hotspots, inspect legality denials, and compare validation runs for complex scenarios.
For phases 2–5 this lab is the umbrella that stitches material truth, live-world truth, and the world-authoring corridor into one lawful production route.

## Material-first world law
This lab owns the world-facing placement and validation corridor for terrain layers, surface families, aftermath overlays, and world-bound material instance stacks.
It may not collapse material law into mesh metadata, shader-only hacks, or imported model trivia.

## Day-zero operator corridor
`project shell -> open or create world -> import heightmap -> sculpt/flatten/paint terrain -> bind surface families -> bind material instance stacks -> apply sky/environment profile -> validate hotspot -> compare -> capture -> recover -> certify`

## Required validation bundles
- `bundle.phase5.world_recipe`
- `bundle.phase5.material_surface_bindings`
- `bundle.phase5.restore_safe_identity`
- `bundle.phase5.launch_ready_region`

## Mandatory surface families for this lab
- terrain base layers;
- biome litter overlays;
- aftermath overlays;
- world-local material instance bindings for authorable objects and terrain patches.

## Validation law
This lab may validate a world package only when every invoked action lowers to a promoted `btn.*` row in `editor/110` or to this lab's explicitly scoped command inventory.
It may not invent parallel world-validation buttons for product relay, save/load, build, launch, or freeze.

## Exit meaning for phase 5
A world package is phase-5-closed in docs only when:
- the region is authorable from this lab and the owning phase-2/3/4 labs;
- compare/capture/recover/certify all round-trip through tooling and sdk;
- terrain layers, surface families, and aftermath overlays remain typed throughout import, save, load, and rebuild;
- restore-safe anchors and mixed-pack attachments are retained as artifacts;
- the region emits one reason backlink into `editor/100` and one evidence backlink into `editor/105`;
- the retained bundle is suitable for freeze, build, export, launch, and first-result verification.
