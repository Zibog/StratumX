# Fur Hair Groom Wind Wetness And Certification Lab Canon

**Stack version:** `SX-CANON/1.0.24/STACK-v30`

## Purpose
Own authoring and certification for fur, hair, and groom families under wind, wetness, char, and old-floor pressure.

## Mandatory tabs
- coverage and groom family
- wind response
- wetness / char variants
- representation ladder
- certification and compare

## Required controls
- bind coverage family and groom family;
- inspect promoted-local strands vs hybrid vs cards;
- inject wind, rain, or burn state;
- inspect silhouette and shadow continuity;
- launch certification capture.

## Required fields
`groom_family_ref`, `coverage_pack_ref`, `wind_response_class`, `wetness_variant_ref`, `char_variant_ref`, `representation_rung`, `silhouette_verdict`
