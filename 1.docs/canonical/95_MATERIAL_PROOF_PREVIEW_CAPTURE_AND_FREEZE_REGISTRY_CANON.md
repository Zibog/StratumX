# Material Proof Preview Capture And Freeze Registry Canon

**Stack version:** `SX-CANON/1.0.28/STACK-v34`

## Purpose
Freeze the registry-grade law for `matresp.proof.*` so material-owned branches are equally strong at preview, compare, capture, retained evidence, and freeze review.

## Master law
Material proof is not optional polish.
When material law is the declared owner, previewability, compare/capture ids, retained artifact posture, and freeze blockers are part of the branch constitution.

## Mandatory proof family buckets
| Family id bucket | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `matresp.proof.preview.*` | operator preview trigger legality |
| `matresp.proof.coverage.*` | branch coverage and missing-binding inspection |
| `matresp.proof.compare.*` | compare triplet and baseline family |
| `matresp.proof.capture.*` | capture bundle family and retained artifact class |
| `matresp.proof.freeze.*` | release-proof blockers and signoff law |

## Required fields
Every proof family row must publish:
- `family_id`
- `normalized_trigger_classes`
- `editor_label_map`
- `compare_mode_family`
- `capture_mode_family`
- `retained_artifact_class`
- `freeze_blocker_family`
- `evidence_row_class`
- `validation_gate_family`
- `focus_target_law`

## Preview label mapping law
Friendly operator labels may exist, but they must map one-way to normalized triggers.
At minimum the archive supports these canonical mappings:
- `Preview Contact` -> `trigger.contact`
- `Preview Scrape` -> `trigger.scrape`
- `Preview Bullet Hit` -> `trigger.ballistic_hit`
- `Preview Blast` -> `trigger.blast_overpressure`
- `Preview Burn` -> `trigger.fire_exposure`
- `Preview Wetness` -> `trigger.wetness_contact`
- `Preview Light Exposure` -> `trigger.light_exposure`

## Freeze law
A material-owned branch is not release-proof if:
- branch coverage is incomplete;
- a required compare/capture family is missing;
- no retained artifact bundle exists for mandatory proof review;
- preview labels exist but do not map to normalized trigger classes.
